US device recovery guide

Lost or stolen device?

Take immediate steps to locate, lock, and report a device you own. Do not attempt to confront a suspected thief or track someone else.

Start recovery checklist
Stay safe

If you see the device or a possible thief, contact local police. Do not take action yourself.

Recovery checklist

What to do now

  1. 1

    Use an official locator

    Sign in only through the manufacturer’s official website.

    Apple Find My ↗ Google Find Hub ↗
  2. 2

    Lock or remotely erase it

    Mark the device lost and lock it with a strong password. Only erase it if you do not need to preserve evidence or continue locating it.

  3. 3

    Contact your carrier

    Ask the carrier to suspend service and help block the device if supported. Keep your account passwords and recovery codes private.

  4. 4

    File a police report

    Record the date, location, serial number, and IMEI if available. Report the theft to local police or your local law-enforcement agency.

    Find local police resources ↗
  5. 5

    Secure your accounts

    Change passwords for email and important accounts, sign out of old sessions, enable multi-factor authentication, and review payment activity.

Device information

Gather these details

  • Device make and model
  • Serial number
  • IMEI or MEID
  • Account email used on the device
  • Date, time, and location of loss or theft

IMEI and MEID are usually in the original box or your carrier account. Do not post these identifiers publicly.

Important

About device cloning

Cloning or bypassing a device without authorization can expose private information and may violate the law. This site does not provide cloning, unlocking, credential, or unauthorized-tracking instructions.

Use recovery tools only on devices you own or are explicitly authorized to manage.
